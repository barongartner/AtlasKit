# News

What goes in the news section of the daily brief. The owner's city comes from setup. Atlas can change the beats here when the owner asks for more or less of something.

## The beats, in this order

One item per beat, two at most on a big day. Skip a beat if nothing real happened in the last 24 to 48 hours, but never skip the whole section.

1. **Local** - the owner's city: roads, weather events, council decisions, transit, crime and safety that made the news, big events, the local teams when something actually happened.
2. **Country** - the one or two national stories everyone will be talking about today, plus anything that changes what things cost.
3. **World** - the biggest international story of the day.
4. **Tech** - the biggest real tech story: major launches, AI models and tools, phones and apps they use. Not a niche research paper or a funding round.

Add beats for their own interests as you learn them (check Goals.md and Memory.md), and note them here.

## How to write it

- One plain line per item, no numbers, no bullets. Say why it matters to them if that isn't obvious.
- Every item gets its real article link. The links go at the very END of the brief, one per line, in the same order as the news lines, with nothing between them. Each link becomes its own bubble with a preview, and text between links makes extra bubbles.
- Since the last brief only. Check the date on every story before you use it, and get the day right.
- Never repeat a story from yesterday's brief. Check yesterday's brief in this session or in Memories\Archive.md first. A follow-up only counts if something new happened.
- The link is the article itself, never a section page or a homepage. If you can't find the article URL, find the story somewhere else or drop it.
- Real outlets only: national and local news organizations, wire services (Reuters, AP), established tech press, or the company's own site. No blogs, aggregators or AI-written sites.
- Pick what they'd actually care about, not whatever ranks first.
